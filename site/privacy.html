This Privacy Policy explains how [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("LiveOpsHub", "we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information in connection with the LiveOpsHub website at [liveopshub.com] and our software application (together, the "Service").

1. Who this applies to

LiveOpsHub is a business tool used by companies ("Customers") to run their live-selling operations. When a Customer uploads order and buyer information into the Service, the Customer is the "controller" of that data and we act as a "processor" on their behalf. This Policy covers information we handle as a business (e.g., account and website data); our handling of Customer-uploaded data is also governed by our agreement with each Customer.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information — name, work email, company name, role, and login credentials.
  • Customer operational data — orders, order items, buyer names, shipping addresses, tracking numbers, and related fulfillment data that Customers import or generate in the Service.
  • Media — packing videos and photos recorded through the Service and associated with orders.
  • Usage & device data — log data, IP address, browser/device type, and actions taken in the Service.
  • Cookies — used to keep you signed in and to operate the Service. See Section 8.

3. How we use information

  • Provide, operate, secure, and improve the Service.
  • Authenticate users and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • Provide customer support and respond to requests.
  • Process billing and manage subscriptions.
  • Send service-related communications (and, with consent, product updates).
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with:

  • Service providers (subprocessors) — e.g., cloud hosting and storage, shipping-label providers, and payment processors, who process data on our behalf under contract.
  • At a Customer's direction — e.g., sending shipment data to a carrier to buy a label.
  • Legal and safety — where required by law or to protect rights, property, or safety.
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

5. Data retention

We retain account data for as long as an account is active and as needed to provide the Service. Packing videos and photos are retained for the period associated with the Customer's plan (e.g., 30 or 90 days) unless a different period is agreed. Customers may request deletion of their data subject to legal retention requirements.

6. Security

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect information, including access controls, encryption in transit, and role-based permissions. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Because much of the data in the Service is controlled by a Customer, please direct such requests to the relevant Customer; we will assist them as required. To exercise rights regarding data we control, contact us at [privacy@liveopshub.com].

8. Cookies
