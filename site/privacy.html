Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Live Ops Hub ("the Service"), operated by Btrade LLC ("we", "us"), helps TikTok Shop sellers run order fulfillment and warehouse operations. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and your rights.

1. Data we collect

  • Shop & order data from TikTok Shop, via the API you authorize: order IDs, line items/SKUs, order status, buyer name and shipping address, package and tracking identifiers, and LIVE-session performance data.
  • Account data for Service users (your staff): name, email, role, and login activity.
  • Operational data we generate: picking/packing status, shipment weights, labels, and tracking numbers.

We access TikTok Shop data only in read mode for the scopes you authorize; we do not write to your TikTok Shop.

2. How we use it

To sync and display orders, group them by LIVE session, drive picking/packing/shipping workflows, generate shipping labels, and provide backups and support. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

4. Data retention

We retain shop and order data while your authorization is active. After you disconnect or deauthorize the app, we delete or anonymize your TikTok Shop data within 30 days, except where longer retention is legally required. Backups age out on a fixed schedule.

5. Deletion after deauthorization

When you deauthorize Live Ops Hub (via TikTok Seller Center or in-app), we stop all data access immediately and begin deletion of your shop data per Section 4. You may also request immediate deletion (see Section 7).

6. Security

Per-seller data isolation, signature-verified webhooks, encrypted credentials and backups, access controls, and session revocation.

7. Your rights (data-subject & deletion requests)

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data, or ask questions about this policy, at ofir@5secbeauty.com. We respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. Account holders can also delete their account in-app.

8. Data location

Data is processed and stored in the United States.

We will post changes here and update the effective date.
