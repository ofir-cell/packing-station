# Device test — TestFlight build 5

First test on real devices. Written 2026-11-10 by device-test-lead, from reading the code. Nobody has run any of this on a phone yet.

- **Who runs it:** Ofir, signed in as himself (his normal account).
- **Devices:** iPhone (Part A, about 10 minutes), then iPad (Part B, about 5 minutes), with the Bluetooth barcode scanner.
- **Company:** the real company. There is no demo company yet.
- **Time:** the minutes above are for tapping and looking. Picking and packing the real orders takes its normal time on top.

## The data is real — read this first

Everything you do in this app happens to real orders.

- Use **only orders that really need to be picked or packed right now**, and pick or pack them for real, exactly as the floor would.
- Never open, tick, finish or film an order just to test.
- Steps marked **[REAL ORDER]** change real data (who is picking, items ticked, order picked, packing video saved, order packed). Do them only on an order you are really working on.
- "Report a problem": in this test you only open the sheet, read it and tap **Cancel**. Tap "Report & skip" only if the order really has a problem.
- Do not seal a box until the screen has shown **Packed** for it.

## Before you start

Have at hand:

- **iPhone and iPad**, both with Live Ops Hub installed from TestFlight.
- **Build number:** open the **TestFlight** app, tap **Live Ops Hub**. It must say **1.0.0 (5)**. The app itself does not show its build number anywhere.
- **iOS version** of each device: Settings › General › About. Write both down now.
- **Bluetooth scanner**, charged, paired with the iPhone in keyboard mode (Settings › Bluetooth). **Keep it switched off for now** — while it is connected, iOS hides the on-screen keyboard. A scanner connects to one device at a time: before Part B, pair it with the iPad.
- **Real orders that are due right now:**
  - iPhone: **2 orders to pick** and **6 boxes to pack**, labels printed.
  - iPad: **1 order to pick** and **3 boxes to pack**.
  - Fewer orders tonight? Do the steps you can and skip the rest. A skipped step stays "not proven"; it is not a failure.
- The iPad stand, if the floor uses one.
- A way to watch a packing video afterwards: the website on a computer, or the Dashboard tab.

## If the app gets stuck

If the camera picture is black or frozen, or "Saving packing video…" stays for more than a minute while the network is on:

1. Write down the step number.
2. Close the app fully (swipe it away), open it again.
3. Record that box again. The first video was not saved.

How to answer: next to each step number write **✓** or **✗** and a few words. A few steps ask "write what you see" with choices A / B / C.

## Part A — iPhone (scanner switched off to start)

### Start and session

1. Open the **TestFlight** app and tap Live Ops Hub.
   Expect: it says **1.0.0 (5)**.

2. Open Live Ops Hub.
   Expect: three tabs at the bottom — **Dashboard, Pick, Pack**. The Dashboard tab shows the website, signed in as you. (If you see the sign-in page instead: sign in, and write "had to sign in".)

3. Close the app fully (swipe it away), then open it again.
   Expect: still signed in. No sign-in page.

4. On the Dashboard tab, scroll your home page to the bottom and open one page from the site menu.
   Expect: both load, and the bottom of the page is not hidden behind the tab bar. (It looks like the desktop website on a small screen. That is known, not a failure.)

### Pick

5. Tap the **Pick** tab.
   Expect: within about 3 seconds — title "Pick", today's date with "… orders waiting", a list called "Shows", and a large button **Scan packing label** fully visible above the tab bar.

6. Tap the show you are really picking now. (Choose one without an orange "to clear" or "Clean table first" tag. Those open the table clean-up page on the desktop site; if that happens, write it down and choose another show.)
   Expect: a short message "Picking <show name>", and a card "You're picking" with that show at the top.

7. Tap **Scan packing label**.
   Expect: the camera opens under the title "Scan". (First time: iOS asks for the camera — allow.)

8. **[REAL ORDER]** Point the camera at the label of the order you are about to pick.
   Expect: the phone buzzes and the order opens — buyer name at the top, the list of items, and a button "… more to pick".

9. Tap the **red triangle button** at the bottom right. Read the sheet. Tap **Cancel**.
   Expect: a sheet "What's wrong?" with four choices (Item missing, Damaged, Wrong SKU on table, Can't find the table), a note box, and buttons Cancel / "Report & skip". After Cancel you are back on the order and nothing was reported.

10. **[REAL ORDER]** Pick the items. Tap each item on the screen as you pull it.
    Expect: each tapped item shows a tick, the line "… of … picked" counts up, and when all are ticked the button changes to **Done — scan next**.

11. **[REAL ORDER]** Tap **Done — scan next**.
    Expect: a green message "Order picked — bring it to the packer", you are back on the list of shows, and "This session" at the bottom shows 1.

12. Switch the scanner **on** and wait until it is connected. Touch nothing on the screen. **[REAL ORDER]** Scan the label of the next order you are picking with the Bluetooth scanner.
    Expect: the order opens by itself, as in step 8.

13. **[REAL ORDER]** Pick it, tick the items, tap **Done — scan next**.
    Expect: same as step 11, "This session" shows 2, and a green line "Scanner ready — scan a label any time" appears on the Pick screen.

### Pack

14. Tap the **Pack** tab.
    Expect: a live camera picture, the words "Scan the shipping label", a box "Tracking number" with a **Go** button, and at the top right a small round record button that is switched on (highlighted). If it is off, tap it once.

15. **[REAL ORDER]** Hold the shipping label of box 1 in front of the camera.
    Expect: the phone buzzes; a card shows the buyer and the items; a red timer at the top left starts counting — recording started by itself. (First time: iOS asks for the microphone — allow.)

16. Compare the card with the real order.

17. **[REAL ORDER]** Pack box 1 in front of the camera, then tap the red **Stop & save**.
    Expect: "Saving packing video…", then a green tick and **Packed** with the tracking number, then after about 2 seconds the scan screen comes back by itself.

18. **[REAL ORDER]** Touch nothing. Scan the label of box 2 with the **Bluetooth scanner**.
    Expect: box 2's card opens and the red timer starts. The camera picture is live (not black, not frozen).

19. **[REAL ORDER]** Pack box 2. Then, still touching nothing, scan the label of **box 3** (the next box).
    Expect: "Saving packing video…" for a moment, then box 3's card opens and the red timer starts again. (The green "Packed" screen may be skipped here. That is fine.)

20. **[REAL ORDER]** Pack box 3. Then scan **box 3's own label** again.
    Expect: "Saving packing video…", then green **Packed**, then the scan screen.

21. Switch the scanner **off**. Tap inside the "Tracking number" box.
    Expect: the on-screen keyboard appears.

## Notes for the agents (from reading the code — not reproduced)

Ofir can skip this section. Each item says where the code suggests a step will not behave as the board assumes.

- **Step 37 / leaving the Pack tab.** `PackFlowView.onDisappear` calls `recorder.shutdown()` (stops the capture session). `PackingRecorder.configure()` only rebuilds when the state is `idle` or `failed`, and nothing else calls `session.startRunning()`. After one recording, leaving Pack for another tab and coming back should leave the recorder `ready` with a stopped session: black preview, and `stop()` may wait forever on its continuation. Owner if seen: ios-engineer.
- **Step 37 / first upload after a new sign-in.** `APIClient` caches the CSRF token from the launch-time `/api/mobile/session`. `uploadPackingVideo` does not refresh the token and retry on a CSRF error (the JSON `send` path does). If the server ties the token to the session, the first upload after signing in without relaunching fails, and the file is deleted. Server side not read. Owner if seen: ios-engineer.
- **Steps 18–19 / second box.** The scan screen runs VisionKit's camera while the recorder's own capture session is still running from the previous box. Whether the recorder's session comes back cleanly for the next box is not knowable from the code.
- **Steps 23–26 / N8a.** `waitsForConnectivity = true` with a 300-second resource timeout: with the network off, a lookup and an upload should wait silently (spinner, no message) for up to 5 minutes and go through if the network returns in time. The recording is deleted (`defer { removeItem }` in `stopAndUpload`) only once the upload actually throws — after the timeout, or when a connection drops mid-upload. So a short outage probably does not lose the video; a long one or a mid-upload drop does. The optional step covers the long case.
- **Steps 34–35 / K7a.** Signed-out Pack shows the normal scan stage (and can raise the camera permission prompt); the signed-out message comes only after a lookup returns 401. Cookies are copied from the web view to the native store only when the Pack tab is selected.
- **Step 30 / P3.** No string catalog in `ios/`; Pack and all native text are English only.
- **Steps 15, 18 / label formats.** `/m/pick` cuts a 23–40 digit USPS barcode down to its last 22 digits before the lookup; native Pack sends the scanned string as it is (after removing non-alphanumeric characters). Whether `/api/shipment/<code>` accepts the long form was not checked. A "Shipment not found" on a camera or scanner scan of a USPS label, where typing the number works, would be this.
- **Typing with a scanner connected.** iOS hides the on-screen keyboard while a keyboard-mode scanner is connected; the script switches the scanner off for the typing steps.
- **Not covered in this round:** a worker or manager login (only Ofir's role), typing a code on Pick, iPad upside-down portrait, a resized iPad window, gloves, a known weak-Wi-Fi spot on the floor.
