Spec: screens/scanit.md · Blueprint S13, flow 4.9. Read-only in 1.0. Tab root for a host; pushed from Today for cs, manager and admin.

Add ?dark=1 for dark mode. Products, prices and notes are invented sample data. The tab bar is the native bar, drawn here only to show the whole phone.

  1. Three inputs: Bluetooth scanner with nothing tapped, "Scan product" (camera), keyboard button (type a barcode or SKU).
  2. "Scanner ready" appears once a hardware scanner has been seen on this device.
  3. The page asks the shell to keep the screen awake while it is in front (bridge, N6).
  1. Drawn scrolled: the large title "Scanit" has moved up into the nav bar.
  2. Both prices at 34px so they read from arm's length. Picture is the placeholder here; a real picture fills the same 112pt square.
  3. A guess never looks like a fact (rule 5.12): the estimated retail price carries the amber "Estimate" pill and the words "Not a checked price". "Set by your team" has no pill.
  4. Source of the notes is text, never a link: /m/ screens do not open other sites.
  5. The next scan replaces the card; earlier products stay in "Scanned this session" (memory only, gone when the app closes).
  6. No edit, no "look up again", no favourites, no name search, no scripts.
  7. For Ofir: "Our target" is as large as the retail price. If the phone can be caught by the live camera, should it be smaller or unlabelled?
  1. Same screen for cs / manager / admin, pushed from Today: a "Today" back button and the Today tab stays selected.
  2. First scan of a barcode the catalog did not know: pill "New — added to the catalog" and where it was found.
  3. A database price is labelled "Store price" with the database's name — it is not the brand's retail price.
  4. "Looking up details…": the card fills in by itself (re-read every 3 s). After 90 s or a failure it reads "No more details found." — no retry button in 1.0.
  1. Offline: grey banner; the last card stays. A scan while offline gets a toast, it is not dropped silently.

  4. Not drawn: the 403 state ("Your account can't open this. Ask your manager.") and the demo banner — both are in the spec.
